Short Questions

QUESTION 1
Find the sum of first 20 natural numbers?
Solution
Use the formula for the sum of the first \( n \) natural numbers: \( S_n = \frac{n(n+1)}{2} \).
Here \( n=20 \), so \( S_{20} = \frac{20(21)}{2} = 10 \times 21 = 210 \).
QUESTION 2
What is the value of \(1^{2}+2^{2}+..........+10^{2}\) ?
Solution
The sum of squares is given by \( \frac{n(n+1)(2n+1)}{6} \).
Substitute \( n=10 \): \( \frac{10(11)(21)}{6} = \frac{2310}{6} = 385 \).
QUESTION 3
What is the unit digit of \(167\times2183\times497\times839\times235\times111\times1039\times251\times563\)
Solution
Observe the factors. We have the number 235, which has a unit digit of 5. All other numbers in the product are odd. Since any odd number multiplied by 5 results in a number ending in 5, the unit digit of the entire product is 5.
QUESTION 4
Find the remainder \(\frac{232\times43\times2347\times4671\times6988}{23}\)
Solution
Find the remainder of each term divided by 23: \( 232 \to 2 \), \( 43 \to -3 \), \( 2347 \to 1 \), \( 4671 \to 2 \), \( 6988 \to -4 \).
Multiply these remainders: \( 2 \times (-3) \times 1 \times 2 \times (-4) = 48 \).
Finally, \( 48 \pmod{23} = 2 \).
QUESTION 5
The number of zeros at the end of 80!
Solution
Count the factors of 5 in 80! using Legendre's Formula: \( \lfloor \frac{80}{5} \rfloor + \lfloor \frac{80}{25} \rfloor \).
\( 16 + 3 = 19 \). There are 19 zeros.
QUESTION 6
Find the value of \(8 \times 88 \times 888\)
Solution
Perform the multiplication directly:
\( 8 \times 88 = 704 \).
\( 704 \times 888 = 625,152 \).
QUESTION 7
Find the LCM of 16, 18, 24 and 30.
Solution
Prime factorization: \( 16=2^4 \), \( 18=2 \cdot 3^2 \), \( 24=2^3 \cdot 3 \), \( 30=2 \cdot 3 \cdot 5 \).
Take the highest power of each prime: \( 2^4 \times 3^2 \times 5^1 = 16 \times 9 \times 5 = 720 \).
QUESTION 8
Find the least number which when divided by 8, 9, 12 and 15 leaves the same remainder 5 in each case
Solution
The number is \( \text{LCM}(8, 9, 12, 15) + \text{Remainder} \).
The LCM is 360. Adding the remainder gives \( 360 + 5 = 365 \).
QUESTION 9
Simplify \(\frac{423\times423-325\times325}{423-325}\)
Solution
This expression matches the form \( \frac{a^2 - b^2}{a - b} \), which simplifies to \( a + b \).
\( 423 + 325 = 748 \).
QUESTION 10
Find Total number of factors \(N=4200\)
Solution
Prime factorization of 4200 is \( 2^3 \cdot 3^1 \cdot 5^2 \cdot 7^1 \).
To find total factors, add 1 to each exponent and multiply:
\( (3+1)(1+1)(2+1)(1+1) = 4 \times 2 \times 3 \times 2 = 48 \).

Long Questions

QUESTION 1
The traffic signals at three different road crossings change after every 30, 45, 50 and 100 seconds respectively. If they all change simultaneously at 11:50 am, then at what time will they again change simultaneously?
Solution
We need to find the LCM of the intervals 30, 45, 50, and 100.
LCM is 900 seconds. Converting 900 seconds to minutes: \( 900/60 = 15 \) minutes.
Adding 15 minutes to the start time 11:50 am gives 12:05 pm.
QUESTION 2
Three wheels can complete 25, 21 and 16 revolutions per minute respectively. There is a red spot on each wheel that touches the ground at time zero. After how much time, all these spots will simultaneously touch the ground again?
Solution
Time for one revolution for each wheel is \( 60/25 \), \( 60/21 \), and \( 60/16 \) seconds.
We need the LCM of these fractions.
\( \text{LCM} = \frac{\text{LCM}(60, 60, 60)}{\text{HCF}(25, 21, 16)} = \frac{60}{1} = 60 \) seconds.
QUESTION 3
7 is added to a certain number and the sum is multiplied by 5. The product is then divided by 3 and 4 is subtracted from the quotient. If the result comes to 16, then what is the original number?
Solution
Work backwards from the result 16:
Add 4: \( 16+4 = 20 \).
Multiply by 3: \( 20 \times 3 = 60 \).
Divide by 5: \( 60 / 5 = 12 \).
Subtract 7: \( 12 - 7 = 5 \). The number is 5.
QUESTION 4
There are some students to be seated in an auditorium. When they were seated in rows of 8,9,11, and 12, there always were 3 students left out to be seated. But when they were seated in 15 rows, non were left. Find the Minium number of student was more than 1000.
Solution
The number \( N \) is of the form \( \text{LCM}(8,9,11,12)k + 3 \).
LCM is 792. So \( N = 792k + 3 \). We need \( N \) divisible by 15 and \( N > 1000 \).
Testing values of \( k \): At \( k=6 \), \( 792(6)+3 = 4755 \).
4755 is divisible by 15 (ends in 5 and sum of digits 21 is divisible by 3).
QUESTION 5
The girls start jogging from the same point around a circular track and each one complects one round in 24 seconds, 36 seconds and 48 seconds respectively. After how much time will they meet at one point?
Solution
Find the LCM of the lap times: 24, 36, and 48.
LCM(24, 36, 48) = 144 seconds. They meet after 144 seconds.
QUESTION 6
The cost of 4 bags and 3 boxes is Rs. 555 and the cost of 3 bags and 4 boxes Rs. 460. What is the cost of bag?
Solution
Equations: \( 4x + 3y = 555 \) and \( 3x + 4y = 460 \).
Multiply first by 3 and second by 4: \( 12x + 9y = 1665 \) and \( 12x + 16y = 1840 \).
Subtracting gives \( 7y = 175 \), so \( y=25 \).
Substitute \( y \) back: \( 4x + 75 = 555 \implies 4x = 480 \implies x = 120 \).
QUESTION 7
There are certain number of benches in a classroom. If four students sit on each bench three benches remain unoccupied. If, however, three students sit on each bench then three students remain standing in the class. Find the number of students in the class?
Solution
Let \( B \) be benches and \( S \) be students.
Case 1: \( S = 4(B-3) \). Case 2: \( S = 3B + 3 \).
Equate them: \( 4B - 12 = 3B + 3 \implies B = 15 \).
Total students: \( 3(15) + 3 = 48 \).
QUESTION 8
In a group 70 people, 37 like coffee, 52 like tea each person likes at least one of the two drinks. Find the number of people who like both coffee and tea.
Solution
Using set theory: \( n(C \cup T) = n(C) + n(T) - n(C \cap T) \).
\( 70 = 37 + 52 - x \implies 70 = 89 - x \).
\( x = 19 \).
QUESTION 9
One-third of the boys and one-half of the girls of a collage participated in a social work project. If the number of participating students in 300 out of which 100 are boys, what is the number of students in the collage?
Solution
Participating Boys = 100. Since this is 1/3 of total boys, Total Boys = 300.
Participating Girls = Total Participants (300) - Boys (100) = 200.
Since 200 is 1/2 of total girls, Total Girls = 400.
Total students = 300 + 400 = 700.
QUESTION 10
The value of \(\sqrt{10+\sqrt{25+\sqrt{108+\sqrt{154+\sqrt{225}}}}}\)
Solution
Solve from inside out:
\( \sqrt{225} = 15 \).
\( 154 + 15 = 169 \to \sqrt{169} = 13 \).
\( 108 + 13 = 121 \to \sqrt{121} = 11 \).
\( 25 + 11 = 36 \to \sqrt{36} = 6 \).
\( 10 + 6 = 16 \to \sqrt{16} = 4 \).

If she spent 69%, she has 31% left.

If the price of one kg of rice is increased by 25%, the increase is Rs 12. Find the new price of rice per kg.
Solution
25% of the original price is Rs 12. So, original price = \( 12 \times 4 = 48 \).
New price = Original + Increase = \( 48 + 12 = 60 \).
QUESTION 5
If A's salary is 20% more than that of B, then how much per cent is B's salary less than that of A?
Solution
Let B = 100. Then A = 120.
Difference is 20. Percentage less = \( \frac{20}{120} \times 100 = \frac{1}{6} \times 100 = 16.66\% \).
QUESTION 6
If the profit made on a packet of tea is Rs 4 and the cost price of the packet is Rs 20, then how much is the profit percentage?
Solution
Profit % = \( \frac{\text{Profit}}{\text{CP}} \times 100 = \frac{4}{20} \times 100 = \frac{1}{5} \times 100 = 20\% \).
QUESTION 7
Harish bought a second-hand typewriter for Rs 1200 and spent Rs 200 on its repairs. He sold it for Rs 1680. What was his profit or loss per cent?
Solution
Total Cost Price = \( 1200 + 200 = 1400 \). Selling Price = 1680.
Profit = \( 1680 - 1400 = 280 \).
Profit % = \( \frac{280}{1400} \times 100 = 20\% \).
QUESTION 8
A dishonest fruit vendor professes to sell his goods at cost price but he uses a weight of 900 g for the kg weight. Find his gain per cent.
Solution
Error = 100g. True Weight = 900g.
Gain % = \( \frac{\text{Error}}{\text{True Weight}} \times 100 = \frac{100}{900} \times 100 = 11.11\% \).
QUESTION 9
50 successive discounts of 2%, 4%, 6%, 8% and so on will in a single discount of?
Solution
The series of discounts is \( 2, 4, 6 \dots \). The 50th term is \( 50 \times 2 = 100\% \).
Since one of the discounts is 100%, the price becomes 0. The single equivalent discount is 100%.
QUESTION 10
Find the single equivalent discount corresponding to the successive discounts of 10% and 15%?
Solution
Formula: \( x + y - \frac{xy}{100} \).
\( 10 + 15 - \frac{150}{100} = 25 - 1.5 = 23.5\% \).

QUESTION 1
We have an article costing Rs. 10000 which is its marked price or tagged price. When we give discount of 10% and 20%, successively on this article, the selling price we get after this?

QUESTION 2

Step 1: \( 12500 \times 1.1 = 13750 \).
Step 2: \( 13750 \times 0.85 = 11687.5 \).

QUESTION 3
In an examination, 33% of the students failed in Maths, 24% failed in English and 17% failed in both. Find the percentage of students who passed in both the subjects.
Solution
Percentage failed in at least one = \( n(M) + n(E) - n(M \cap E) \).
\( 33 + 24 - 17 = 40\% \).
Passed in both = \( 100 - 40 = 60\% \).
QUESTION 4
Find a single equivalent increase, if a number is successively increased by 10%, 20% and 30%.

QUESTION 5
In an examination 35% of the students failed in Math, 25% of the students failed in English, 45% of the students failed in Hindi. If 10% of the students failed in Math and English, 20% of the students failed in English and Hindi, 30% of the students failed in Math and Hindi and 5% of the students failed in all the three subjects Math, English and Hindi, then find the percentage of students who passed in all three subjects.
Solution
Use inclusion-exclusion for failures:
\( n(A \cup B \cup C) = \sum n(A) - \sum n(A \cap B) + n(A \cap B \cap C) \).
\( (35+25+45) - (10+20+30) + 5 = 105 - 60 + 5 = 50\% \).
Passed all = \( 100 - 50 = 50\% \).
QUESTION 6
A man buys 3 tables and 12 chairs for Rs 2400. He sells the tables at a profit of 20% and chairs at a profit of 10% and makes a total profit of Rs 300. At what price did he buy tables and chairs?
Solution
Let \( T \) and \( C \) be cost per item.
1) \( 3T + 12C = 2400 \).
2) \( 3T(0.20) + 12C(0.10) = 300 \implies 0.6T + 1.2C = 300 \implies T + 2C = 500 \).
From (1), \( T + 4C = 800 \). Subtracting: \( 2C = 300 \implies C = 150 \).
\( T + 300 = 500 \implies T = 200 \). Cost: Table Rs 200, Chair Rs 150.
QUESTION 7
2/3 of a commodity is sold at 30% profit, 1/4 is sold at 16% profit and the rest at 12% profit. If a total profit of Rs 75 is earned, then find the value of the commodity.

25% of Value = 75.
Value = \( \frac{75}{25} \times 100 = 300 \).

QUESTION 8

Long Questions

QUESTION 1
A man covers a certain distance between his house and office on scooter. Having an average speed of \(60~km/hr.\) he is late by 20 min. However, with a speed of 80km / hr. he reaches his office 10 min earlier. Find the distance between his house and office.
Solution
Time difference = 30 min = 0.5 hr.
\( \frac{D}{60} - \frac{D}{80} = 0.5 \).
\( \frac{4D-3D}{240} = 0.5 \implies D = 120 \) km.
QUESTION 2
Two men A and B walk from P to Q, a distance of 22 km, at 5 and 6 km an hour respectively. B reaches Q, returns immediately and meets A at R. Find the distance from P to R.
Solution
Let distance PR be \( x \). A travels \( x \), B travels \( 22 + (22-x) = 44-x \).
Time is same: \( \frac{x}{5} = \frac{44-x}{6} \).
\( 6x = 220 - 5x \implies 11x = 220 \implies x = 20 \) km.
QUESTION 3
Without any stoppage a person travels a certain distance at an average speed of \(40~km/hr.\), and with stop-pages he covers the same distance at an average speed of \(30~km/hr\) How many minutes per hour does he stop?
Solution
Loss in distance due to stopping = \( 40 - 30 = 10 \) km.
Time to cover 10 km at 40 km/h = \( \frac{10}{40} \times 60 = 15 \) minutes.
QUESTION 4
A policeman goes after a thief who has 100 meters' start. If the policeman runs a kilometer in 8 minutes, and the thief a kilometer in 10 minutes, how far will the thief have gone before he is overtaken?
Solution
P Speed = 125 m/min. T Speed = 100 m/min. Relative = 25 m/min.
Time to close 100m = 4 min.
Distance Thief = \( 100 \times 4 = 400 \) meters.
QUESTION 5
Ram travels at the rate of \(3~km/hr\). and he reaches 15 minutes late. If he travels at the rate of \(4km/hr.\) he reaches 15 minutes earlier. The distance Ram has to travel is
Solution
Difference 30 min = 0.5 hr.
\( \frac{D}{3} - \frac{D}{4} = 0.5 \implies \frac{D}{12} = 0.5 \implies D = 6 \) km.
QUESTION 6
Ramesh travel by bus from city A to city B at an average speed of \(44km/hr.\) Suresh travel by taxi from city A to city B at an average speed of \(77km/hr\) and takes 6 hours lesser than time taken by Ramesh. What is the distance (in km) between the two cities?
Solution
\( \frac{D}{44} - \frac{D}{77} = 6 \).
\( D(\frac{7-4}{308}) = 6 \implies 3D = 1848 \implies D = 616 \) km.
QUESTION 7
A motor car does a journey in 12 hr. the first half at \(15~km/hr\) and the second half at \(30~km/hr\). Find the distance.
Solution
Let total dist be \( D \).
\( \frac{D/2}{15} + \frac{D/2}{30} = 12 \).
\( \frac{D}{30} + \frac{D}{60} = 12 \implies \frac{3D}{60} = 12 \implies D = 240 \) km.
QUESTION 8
A man takes 6 hrs. 30 min in walking to a certain place and riding back. He would have gained 2 hrs. 10 min by riding both ways. How long would he take to walk both ways?
Solution
W + R = 6h 30m.
2R = 6h 30m - 2h 10m = 4h 20m.
2W = 2(W+R) - 2R = 13h - 4h 20m = 8h 40m.
QUESTION 9
A train running at 35 km per hour takes 18 seconds to pass a platform. Next, it takes 12 seconds to pass a man walking at the rate of \(5~km/hr\). in the same direction. Find the length of the train and that of the platform.
Solution
Relative Speed (Man) = 30 km/h = 25/3 m/s.
Train Length = \( \frac{25}{3} \times 12 = 100 \) m.
Platform Speed = 35 km/h = 350/36 m/s.
\( 100 + P = 35 \times \frac{5}{18} \times 18 = 175 \).
\( P = 75 \) m.
QUESTION 10
A boat running upstream takes 10 hours to cover a certain distance, while it takes 7 hours to cover the same distance running downstream. What is the ratio between the speed of the boat and that of the water current respectively?
Solution
\( B-W = D/10 \), \( B+W = D/7 \).
\( B = \frac{1}{2}(\frac{D}{7} + \frac{D}{10}) = \frac{17D}{140} \).
\( W = \frac{1}{2}(\frac{D}{7} - \frac{D}{10}) = \frac{3D}{140} \).
Ratio 17:3.
